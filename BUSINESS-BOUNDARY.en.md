# NeedConcord Protocol: commercial and protocol boundaries

[简体中文](BUSINESS-BOUNDARY.md) | **English**

These boundaries also apply to the retained `ODIP/0.1.3-exp.1` wire protocol. The public-name change does not change them.

1. The protocol is free to use and does not require a particular platform or designated settlement account. Using ODIP does not automatically create a payment obligation to the protocol's creator.
2. Platforms may set prices for optional value-added services such as product discovery, understanding needs, coordinating proposals and coordinating fulfillment.
3. The proposed platform rate of **0.35%** is only a commercial idea for a matching-service fee. Its calculation base and service responsibilities must be disclosed. It must not be presented as an industry-wide standard fee or as including all logistics and payment costs.
4. A matching fee is charged once per chargeable transaction unit, not repeatedly according to the number of buyers, factories, carriers, insurers or Agents involved. Additional, independent service transactions may have separate charges, but must be clearly distinguished to prevent repeated commissions on the original order value.
5. Agent channel revenue sharing, such as 0.20% for the platform and 0.15% for an Agent, requires actual contracts, authorized commercial APIs and confirmed transaction events. It does not automatically apply to independently deployed nodes.
6. A completed transaction does not necessarily mean a need has been met. The absence of a transaction does not mean no value has been created.
7. Matching and ranking results should disclose sponsorship, commission effects and data coverage. Actual fairness still requires verification of the platform's algorithms, audits and operating processes.
8. Any insights sold externally should be lawfully obtained, adequately protected aggregates. Selling individual users' original demand text, email addresses or identity data must not be the default.
