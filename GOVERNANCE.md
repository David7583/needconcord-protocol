# 治理规则 / Governance

NeedConcord Protocol 初期采用维护者主导、过程开放的治理方式；当前不存在已成立的基金会、标准委员会或第三方认证机构。本文件定义社区参与流程，不代表已有贡献者规模或第三方认可。

NeedConcord Protocol begins with maintainer-led, open governance. It is not an established foundation, standards committee or certification body. This document defines participation processes without claiming an existing contributor base or third-party endorsement.

## 不可由商业控制改写的原则 / Protected principles

需求优先、角色中立、表达不等于采购承诺、需求满足与交易状态独立、商品/服务/定制/非交易方案并存、无强制中心或平台账户、协议免费且无强制协议税、保留原话并区分推断确认与事实、隐私和开放互操作，均是评审约束。赞助、渠道收入或实现便利不能替代这些约束。

Reviews must preserve demand-first and role-neutral collaboration, expression without purchase commitment, independent need and transaction states, multiple solution paths, no mandatory center or account, a free protocol without mandatory protocol tax, original wording and distinct evidence levels, privacy and open interoperability. Sponsorship, channel revenue and implementation convenience do not supersede these principles.

## 决策过程 / Decisions

1. 提交可复现问题或 RFC，声明动机、替代方案、反例、兼容性与安全隐私影响。Submit an issue or RFC with motivation, alternatives, counterexamples, compatibility and security/privacy effects.
2. 核心字段、规范性状态语义、身份信任或版本兼容规则变更，必须公开讨论，记录主要支持与反对意见，并留出合理回应时间；紧急安全修复可先保密协调，修复后公开经脱敏的说明。Discuss core field, state, trust and compatibility changes openly, preserve material objections and allow reasonable response time. Coordinated security fixes may remain private until a sanitized disclosure is appropriate.
3. 维护者检查规范、Schema、公开测试与实现证据，作出接受、修改后接受、推迟或拒绝决定，并记录理由。Maintainers review specification, schemas, tests and implementation evidence and record acceptance, revision, deferral or rejection with reasons.
4. 破坏性变更必须有新实验版本标识、迁移说明与兼容性测试；不能靠改测试掩盖规范倒退。Breaking changes require a new experimental identifier, migration notes and compatibility tests. Tests must not conceal a regression in protocol goals.

投票、表情反应和讨论人数用于了解意见，不自动修改规范，也不把数量当作技术证据。只有经过审核、合并并明确版本的规范文本构成候选变化。维护者意见也须接受反例与独立证据检验。

Votes, reactions and participant counts inform discussion; they do not amend the protocol automatically or replace technical evidence. Only reviewed, merged, versioned text changes the candidate. Maintainer views remain open to counterexamples and independent evidence.

## 利益冲突与责任 / Conflicts and accountability

发起者与初始维护者为 David7583。维护者应披露与讨论相关的雇佣、赞助、商业分成或实现利益；直接存在利益冲突时应邀请无直接利益者复核，并在决策记录说明处理。任何公司赞助都不自动赋予否决权。新增维护者与权限范围须公开说明；当前不虚构其他维护者、贡献者或合作机构。

David7583 is the project initiator and initial maintainer. Maintainers should disclose relevant employment, sponsorship, revenue-sharing and implementation interests. Seek review without the direct conflict and record its handling. Sponsorship confers no automatic veto. Announce new maintainers and their permissions; no other maintainers, contributors or partners are invented here.

不同意见可附证据请求重新审议。重大未解决分歧应保留为开放问题，而不是宣传为已达成行业共识。未来形成独立贡献群体后，可以通过同样的公开流程提议治理调整。

Dissenters may request reconsideration with evidence. Record unresolved disagreements rather than claiming industry consensus. A future independent contributor community may propose governance changes through this same process.
